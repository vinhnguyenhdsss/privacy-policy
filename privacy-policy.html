Privacy Policy - Tower Defense

Effective Date: November 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game Tower Defense (the "App") developed and published by EdWoodVinhNguyen ("we", "us", or "our").

We respect your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights. The App is a free-to-play entertainment game. We do not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, email, or phone number.

1. Information We Collect

When you use the App, the following information may be collected automatically (primarily through Google AdMob and Google Play Services):

  • Advertising Identifier (Google Advertising ID / IDFA)
  • Device information (device model, operating system version, screen resolution, language, etc.)
  • IP address
  • Usage data (play time, levels played, game progress, interactions with the App and advertisements)
  • Crash logs and diagnostic information
  • Approximate location (derived from IP address, not precise GPS)

We do not collect sensitive personal information such as your real name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, or payment details (unless you make in-app purchases processed by Google Play / Apple).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To display personalized and non-personalized advertisements via Google AdMob
  • To measure ad performance and frequency
  • To improve gameplay, fix bugs, and optimize the App
  • For analytics and performance monitoring
  • To detect and prevent fraud or abuse

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob – for displaying advertisements
  • Google Play Services – for Android functionality and advertising support

These services may collect and process the data listed above according to their own privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • AdMob Privacy Policy

Data collected by AdMob may be shared with Google and its advertising partners for the purpose of serving and measuring ads.

4. Advertising and Your Choices

The App displays ads through Google AdMob. These may be personalized or non-personalized based on your consent and device settings.

You can control personalized advertising:

  • Android: Go to Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization
  • iOS: Go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → turn off “Allow Apps to Request to Track”

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, and Switzerland, we use Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request consent where required by law.

5. Data Sharing and Sale

We do not sell your personal information.

We only share data with the third-party services mentioned above (mainly Google AdMob) as necessary to provide advertisements and operate the App.

6. International Data Transfers

Your information may be processed on servers located outside your country of residence (including the United States). Google applies appropriate safeguards for such transfers.
